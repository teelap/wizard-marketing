Alice Video Assistant

Terms of use

Alice Video Assistant is operated by Jake Tlapek, Jake the Wizard, for his private use. There is no public account registration, subscription or customer service offering through this app.

Current features

The app helps Jake find files in his configured Google Drive library, save selected originals or supported exports, and inspect his configured YouTube channel and its video metadata. Saved files may be reused for tasks he authorizes. The video connection does not change original files or sharing permissions, download YouTube media, retrieve YouTube transcripts, upload, edit or publish content.

Jake controls the accounts he authorizes and must have the rights needed for the files and uses he requests. A connected account does not establish ownership of every file or permission to publish it. Service availability, Google access, and supported sizes and formats can limit a task.

YouTube and privacy

By using the app's YouTube features, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The app uses YouTube API Services. Google describes its practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's privacy policy describes the data accessed, assistant providers, saved copies and removal controls. Google authorization and deletion of previously retained copies are separate actions.

Control and questions

Jake can use the video disconnect control or withdraw access through Google's account permissions settings. Disconnecting does not delete all saved files or conversation histories; those require separate removal as described in the privacy policy.

For assistance or privacy requests, contact jake@jakethewizard.com.
