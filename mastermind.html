The House

One room. Two ways in.

Everything I teach now lives in one place, under one name. The book is Eight Dominoes. The room is the Eight Dominoes Mastermind. Same framework, same order, same language — so nothing you learn in one has to be re-translated for the other.

The free side is open to anyone. Walk in, read the Start Here post, say what you sell and what's stuck. People answer. It costs nothing and I'm not going to make you sit through a call to get it.

Membership is where I'm actually in the room with you — twice a week, live, working on your numbers rather than talking about mine. Plus the full course library and my WhatsApp.

You bring real numbers, real problems, and the willingness to hear the truth and act on it. That part hasn't changed.

Margin note from the WizardThe idea is older than the buzzword. Napoleon Hill documented the “master mind alliance” in 1937 after two decades studying Andrew Carnegie's circle — coordinated minds, borrowed pattern recognition. The tools changed. The mechanism didn't.

Week One

Your first week.

Most communities go quiet the moment you pay. Here's the actual first week of a membership trial.

  1. 1

    The minute you're in

    You land on Start Here, not on an empty feed. It tells you where everything is and what the room is for, in about ninety seconds of reading.

  2. 2

    Your first post

    The onboarding list asks you to introduce yourself in Ask the Room: what you sell, who you sell it to, and the one thing that's stuck right now. One paragraph is plenty. That post is what gets you useful answers instead of polite hellos — people reply to a specific problem, not to “excited to be here.”

  3. 3

    The course library, immediately

    All 96 lessons are open on day one. Most people start with the Eight Dominoes track and work all eight against their own offer, because that's the vocabulary everything else on the calls assumes.

  4. 4

    Your first call

    Whichever comes first, Tuesday or Thursday, 11am Arizona. You do not have to take a hot seat on day one — plenty of people watch two before they put their own numbers up, and watching someone else get taken apart is most of the value anyway.

  5. 5

    Day seven

    The trial ends and the first payment runs. If it wasn't worth it, cancel before then and you've paid nothing. If it was, my WhatsApp number arrives in your inbox that day.

Straight Talk

Don't pay me yet.

There's no application any more and there's no gate on the front door, so I'll say the quiet part here instead: $500 a month is wrong for a lot of people, and I'd rather tell you now than take a month's money to find out together.

It's worth it for a founder or marketing leader who has crossed the starting line — real product, real customers, real problems — and who will actually show up on a Tuesday and put their numbers on the screen. The calls are the product. Skip them and you're paying for a course library.

If you're pre-revenue, still deciding what you sell, or you know you won't make the calls: join free and stay free. Read the room, use the Book Readers space, come back when the calls would pay for themselves. The free side isn't a trial that runs out.

Margin note from the WizardThe free tier isn't a loss leader I'm tolerating. A room where only paying members can speak turns into a room where nobody says anything that might look stupid — and the stupid-looking question is usually the one worth answering. The free side keeps the traffic honest.

FAQ

Before you ask.

What does it cost?

Joining is free and stays free. Membership is $500 a month, or $5,000 a year if you'd rather pay once and get two months free. Either way it starts with seven days free and nothing due today, and you can cancel any time from inside the room.

What's the difference between free and paid?

Free gets you the feed, Start Here, Ask the Room and Book Readers — the parts where people talk to each other. Membership adds the parts where I'm in it: group consulting twice a week, all six course tracks, the members' lounge, and my WhatsApp. You can see the locked spaces from the free side, so you know exactly what you're deciding about.

When are the calls, and what happens on them?

Tuesdays and Thursdays at 11am Arizona — and Arizona doesn't do daylight saving, so check what that is where you are twice a year. Hot seats and case reviews. Somebody puts a real problem on the screen and we work it. Recordings go up for the members who couldn't make it, but the value is being in the seat.

Do I need to read the book first?

No. Eight Dominoes is the framework the room runs on, and there's a space for people who arrive from it — but the Eight Dominoes course track covers the same ground from domino one. The book is the cheapest way to find out whether you like how I think.

How many people are in there?

Not many yet, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. The room reopened on Circle in September 2026 and I moved everything across — all six course tracks, all 96 lessons, the calls on the calendar. What I don't have yet is a wall of member testimonials, because the people who'd write them only just walked in. If a big number is what would convince you, wait six months and check back. If getting me on a call twice a week before the room fills up sounds better than being member 4,000, come now.
