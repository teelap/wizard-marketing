COOKIE
POLICY.

What gets stored in your browser, and how to control it.

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how jakethewizard.com (the “Site”), operated by Jake Tlapek, uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with the Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website (or a third-party service it uses) stores in your browser. They’re widely used to make sites work, to remember preferences, and to understand how a site is used. “Similar technologies” include things like localStorage, which I use to remember that you’ve seen the cookie notice.

The cookies this Site uses

The Site itself is intentionally light. The main categories are:

CategoryPurposeSet by
Essential Remember your cookie-notice choice and keep the Site secure and functioning. The Site can’t work properly without these. This Site (first-party)
Embedded media When a page embeds a video or social post, the provider may set cookies to deliver content and measure engagement. YouTube, TikTok
Scheduling If you book a call, the scheduling provider may set cookies to run the booking flow. Calendly
Analytics Understand how the Site is used — pages viewed, traffic sources, and which actions convert — so I can improve it. Governed by your consent and Google’s Consent Mode. Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager
Advertising & measurement Measure how my advertising performs and reach relevant audiences on Facebook and Instagram. The Meta Pixel and Conversions API record key actions (such as form submissions and bookings) and match them to Meta accounts using hashed identifiers. Active by default outside the EEA/UK; off until you Accept inside the EEA/UK; and switched off anywhere if you Decline. Meta (Facebook) Pixel & Conversions API
Delivery & fonts Serving fonts, icons, and scripts efficiently via a content-delivery network. Google Fonts, CDN

Third-party cookies are governed by the providers that set them. I don’t control their cookies and recommend reviewing their policies for specifics.

Advertising & measurement

I use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Meta’s Conversions API to measure how my advertising performs and to reach relevant audiences on Facebook and Instagram. When you take a key action — such as submitting a form or starting a booking — that event is shared with Meta together with hashed identifiers (for example, a hashed version of your email) used only to match the action to a Meta account. I do not sell your personal information. Outside the EEA and UK these tools are on by default; you can switch them off at any time by choosing Decline on the cookie notice. Inside the EEA and UK they stay off until you choose Accept.

How to control cookies

You’re in control. You can:

  • Use the Decline option on the cookie notice to record that preference.
  • Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Most browsers let you do this per-site or globally — see the help pages for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
  • Block third-party content (such as embedded video) using your browser’s privacy controls or an extension. Note that blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the Site work.

Changes to this policy

I may update this Cookie Policy as the Site’s tools change. When I do, I’ll revise the “Last updated” date above.
