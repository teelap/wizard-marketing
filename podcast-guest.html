Jake's bio.

Copy and paste this for your show notes, episode page, or wherever you introduce your guests.

Jake Tlapek, known as The Wizard of Marketing, has spent 13 years fixing what quietly kills growth: messaging that sells the product instead of the future the buyer wants. He has generated over $100 million in measurable revenue for clients and made two exits: Brio Studio and Wizard Marketing, an SEO and web shop he built over seven years and then sold. Today he runs an independent consulting practice and The Eight Dominoes Mastermind.

He is the author of Eight Dominoes: A Messaging Framework That Compounds Into Awesome Clients (2026), the book on the psychology of buying behind his biggest client results.
