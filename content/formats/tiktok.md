**Reads with:** [platforms/tiktok.md](../platforms/tiktok.md) (cadence/algorithm/specs/SEO — every "why it works" anchors there) · [README.md](README.md) (schema, tiers, dealing rules) · [CONTENT_GUIDE](../CONTENT_GUIDE.md) · [VOICE_DOSSIER](../../VOICE_DOSSIER.md) (§3 has Jake's verbatim captions — already-proven hooks) · [CONTENT_MATRIX](../CONTENT_MATRIX.md).

> Start from a card, then run the QA gate (format → voice → stop-slop). The hook = on-screen text + spoken first line (clips) + first 150 caption chars, all carrying the SEO keyword. Voice is lowercase, plainspoken teacher, mild deadpan, one takeaway (CONTENT_GUIDE §11; imitate the VOICE_DOSSIER §3 samples). No emoji, no exclamation marks, no em-dashes. Numbers only from CONTENT_GUIDE §2, never first-person. CTAs are in-world imperatives. Video cards are `***SHOOT VIDEO***` scripts for Jake to film.

## 1. Format cards

### The One-Concept Carousel — Tier A
- **What:** an 8-slide photo carousel that teaches one messaging concept, symptom-first, ending on the CTA.
- **Why it works here:** carousels are TikTok's highest-leverage format (~81% more engagement, per tiktok.md); each swipe is its own dwell signal, and completion rate is the primary ranking input, so a keyworded slide 1 + caption wins TikTok SEO across all three index layers (audio, on-screen text, first 150 caption chars).
- **Skeleton:** slide 1 = SEO-keyworded symptom hook ("the messaging mistake costing you customers") · 2 = name the real problem · 3–6 = the fix in beats, one idea per slide · 7 = the proof (a stat-bank number, framed as "a client") · 8 = the CTA (consulting "Book the call", the relevant blog post, or the newsletter — book/waitlist CTA paused for now, CONTENT_GUIDE §3). Keyword lives in slide-1 text + caption first 150 chars (silent carousel = nothing spoken); 3–5 hashtags.
- **Hooks that fit:** the callout, the contrarian claim, the number cold-open.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** slide 1: "the messaging mistake quietly costing you customers." caption: "how to fix your value proposition before it loses the sale. one fix at a time. #marketing #messaging #smallbusiness #copywriting"
- **Don't:** a pretty cover with a vague title. slide 1 must name the wound and carry the keyword. don't save the payoff for slide 8 only; each middle slide earns the next swipe.
- **Best for:** Pitfall, Method, Myth. Subjects: the messaging concepts, one at a time (taught plainly, not labeled "Dominoes" — CONTENT_GUIDE §8).

### The Count Carousel ("5 X mistakes") — Tier A
- **What:** a numbered-list carousel, one item per slide, cataloging the mistakes (or fixes) in a topic Jake owns.
- **Why it works here:** the list tease is one of the most consistently viral hook patterns in 2026 (a clear promise + a curiosity gap — "which one am I making"); the number on slide 1 sets the swipe count, and the count itself is the completion engine that feeds the completion-over-views signal (tiktok.md).
- **Skeleton:** slide 1 = number + keyworded promise ("5 marketing mistakes killing your conversion") · slides 2–6 = one mistake each, named in 4–6 words then the one-line fix · final slide = the one that matters most + CTA. Caption repeats the keyword in the first 150 chars; the odd/specific number stays in slide-1 text.
- **Hooks that fit:** the list tease, the mistake warning, the number cold-open.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** slide 1: "5 messaging mistakes quietly killing your conversion rate." slide 2: "describing the service instead of the wound. fix the headline first." caption: "five messaging mistakes most small businesses make, and how to fix each one. #marketing #copywriting #smallbusiness #conversion"
- **Don't:** pad to hit a round number. five real mistakes beat seven with filler. don't make every slide read the same shape — vary mistake-then-fix vs fix-then-why.
- **Best for:** Pitfall, Tip, Method. Subjects: CRO, messaging, TikTok SEO, DSP.

### The Teardown Carousel ("here's what was broken") — Tier A
- **What:** a before/after carousel that diagnoses one real (or composite) piece of marketing, shows what was broken, then the fix.
- **Why it works here:** showing the result/contrast early is the single highest-performing hook type; a visible before/after gives the eye an instant payoff frame, and the diagnosis-first structure is Jake's native voice (VOICE_DOSSIER §2.1). tiktok.md lists client teardowns as a primary repurpose source.
- **Skeleton:** slide 1 = the broken artifact + keyworded verdict ("this homepage is losing the sale in the first line") · 2 = circle the wound · 3 = why it costs money (stakes) · 4–6 = the rewrite, beat by beat · 7 = the result (stat-bank number as "a client") · 8 = CTA. Keyword in slide-1 text + caption.
- **Hooks that fit:** the callout, the outcome showcase, the contrarian claim.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** slide 1: "this is what a broken value proposition looks like." caption: "here's what was broken about this homepage, and the rewrite that fixed it. a client went from 1.2% to 6.5% on a change this small. #marketing #cro #copywriting #conversion"
- **Don't:** mock a named real brand or invent a client number. composite teardowns are fine; fabricated stats are not (CONTENT_MATRIX §3 guardrail).
- **Best for:** Story, Pitfall, Method. Subjects: CRO, messaging, value proposition, the button story.

### The Myth-Bust Carousel — Tier A
- **What:** a carousel that states a belief the audience holds, kills it on slide 1, and replaces it across the swipes.
- **Why it works here:** the contrarian open works because the brain cannot leave a contradiction unresolved, so the swipe-through is involuntary; it maps onto Jake's truth-teller positioning (CONTENT_GUIDE §5.5) and the Myth Topic Type. The contradiction in slide-1 text is also a clean keyword carrier.
- **Skeleton:** slide 1 = the myth, stated then struck ("a new logo will fix slow sales. it won't") · 2 = why everyone believes it · 3 = what's actually true · 4–6 = the real fix in beats · 7 = the proof · 8 = CTA. Keyword inside the myth line + caption first 150 chars.
- **Hooks that fit:** the myth-correction, the contrarian claim, the callout.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** slide 1: "a rebrand will fix your slow sales. it won't." caption: "the rebrand myth, and what actually fixes slow sales. it was never the logo. #marketing #branding #smallbusiness #messaging"
- **Don't:** bust a myth you can't replace. if slide 1 says "it won't," slides 3–6 owe the audience what does. don't strawman; name a belief people actually hold.
- **Best for:** Myth, Commentary, Takeaway. Subjects: branding, AI-skepticism, Share of Brand Voice, personal brand.

### The Diagnosis Clip — Tier A `***SHOOT VIDEO***`
- **What:** a 21–34s talking-head where Jake names a symptom, diagnoses the real cause, gives one proof, lands one takeaway.
- **Why it works here:** tiktok.md ranks talking-head diagnosis clips #2 and notes "Jake's deadpan voice travels"; the expert-explainer hook (authority → curiosity gap → payoff) telegraphs expertise in 3 seconds, and spoken audio lets the keyword hit all three SEO layers at once. Watch-time on a tight 30s clip clears the completion bar more easily than a padded one.
- **Skeleton:** 0–3s = on-screen text + spoken hook carrying the keyword (the diagnosis line) · 3–12s = name the real problem · 12–22s = one proof (a stat-bank number as "a client," or an aphorism) · 22–30s = one takeaway + verbal CTA. Say the keyword in the first 3 seconds and pin it as on-screen text; repeat in caption first 150 chars.
- **Hooks that fit:** the diagnosis two-beat, the expert explainer, the callout.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** on-screen + first line: "your product isn't the problem. your sequence is." caption: "most slow sales aren't a product problem. they're a sequence problem. here's how to tell. #marketing #messaging #smallbusiness"
- **Don't:** open with "hey guys" or "okay so" or a slow windup. the diagnosis is the first words out of his mouth. don't run past 34s without a reason; padding tanks completion.
- **Best for:** Takeaway, Pitfall, Commentary, FAQ. Subjects: any messaging concept, Share of Brand Voice, AI-skepticism.

### The FAQ Answer Clip — Tier B `***SHOOT VIDEO***`
- **What:** a 21–30s talking-head that answers one question Jake actually gets, framed as the question on screen.
- **Why it works here:** a literal question is a built-in search query, so it pulls discovery from TikTok search (1 in 4 sessions start with search, per tiktok.md); answering in one tight beat keeps completion high. B-tier because it lands best when the question is genuinely common, otherwise it reads invented.
- **Skeleton:** 0–3s = the question as on-screen text + spoken ("do i need more traffic or better conversion") · 3–8s = the short answer, stated flat · 8–22s = the why, one beat · 22–30s = takeaway + CTA. The question IS the keyword string; mirror it in the caption first 150 chars.
- **Hooks that fit:** the question-as-hook, the contrarian claim, the diagnosis two-beat.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** on-screen + first line: "do you need more traffic, or better conversion." caption: "the question i get most: more traffic or better conversion. usually it's the second one. here's why. #marketing #cro #conversion #smallbusiness"
- **Don't:** answer a question nobody asks. don't bury the answer; say it by second 8, then explain.
- **Best for:** FAQ, Method, Takeaway. Subjects: CRO, TikTok SEO, DSP, pricing.

### The Text-on-Screen Tip — Tier B
- **What:** a faceless 11–18s clip: one practical tip as on-screen text over b-roll or a static frame, ambient sound.
- **Why it works here:** tiktok.md ranks text-on-screen #3 and confirms faceless works; it's the fastest unit to ship from the AI pipeline when Jake can't film, and a short tip clip completes easily. The keyword in the on-screen text gets OCR-indexed even with no spoken audio.
- **Skeleton:** frame 1 (0–2s) = keyworded tip headline as the hook ("rewrite your headline as the wound") · middle = the tip in one or two more text beats · end = "ship it today" + CTA. Add a trending-but-quiet audio track for distribution (tiktok.md). Keyword in the opening text + caption first 150 chars.
- **Hooks that fit:** the blunt imperative, the tip headline, the number cold-open.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** opening text: "stop describing your service. describe the wound." caption: "one headline fix you can ship today: write the wound, not the service. #marketing #copywriting #smallbusiness #messaging"
- **Don't:** wall of text. one idea per frame, big enough to read in the safe area (keep out of the bottom 15%). don't skip the audio track; silent faceless posts get throttled.
- **Best for:** Tip, Method, Takeaway. Subjects: messaging, CRO, benefit-of-the-benefit, TikTok SEO.

### The Aphorism Card — Tier B
- **What:** a 1–3 slide carousel (or static card) built around one of Jake's verbatim aphorisms, unpacked plainly.
- **Why it works here:** rhyme-and-cadence lines read as more true (VOICE_DOSSIER Fact #36) and Jake's aphorisms are already proven, quotable lines (CONTENT_GUIDE §7); a quote card is high-save, and saves weigh heavily (tiktok.md). B-tier because it teaches less than a full concept — a quick riff, not a launch driver.
- **Skeleton:** slide 1 = the aphorism, clean, as the hook · slide 2 = what it means in two beats · slide 3 (optional) = how to use it today + CTA. Caption carries the topical keyword the aphorism implies, in the first 150 chars.
- **Hooks that fit:** the aphorism, the contrarian claim, the takeaway.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** slide 1: "people browse intellectually and buy emotionally." caption: "twenty years in, this is the line that changed how i write every offer. here's what it means for your copy. #marketing #copywriting #branding"
- **Don't:** stack three aphorisms on one card; one line, room to breathe. don't invent a new aphorism — use the §7 list verbatim.
- **Best for:** Takeaway, Commentary, Myth. Subjects: emotion-buys, AI-skepticism, personal brand, revenue-as-metric.

### The News-React Clip — Tier C `***SHOOT VIDEO***`
- **What:** a 21–45s "Hold My Beard mode" reaction to a current marketing/SEO/AI story, with Jake's POV and a joke.
- **Why it works here:** reacting to a live story rides existing search and trend demand, and Hold-My-Beard commentary is a documented Jake mode (CONTENT_GUIDE §4.9). C-tier and trend-driven by nature: timely, lower-evergreen, fits the ~30% experimental quota and the daily-reactive layer.
- **Skeleton:** 0–3s = the news + Jake's verdict in one breath, keyword = the topic ("google's new update isn't the problem you think it is") · 3–20s = what actually changed · 20–35s = what to do about it · 35–45s = takeaway + CTA. Say the topic keyword early; mirror in caption first 150 chars; add a trending hashtag alongside the evergreen 3–5.
- **Hooks that fit:** the news react, the contrarian claim, the diagnosis two-beat.
- **Example (Jake's voice):** on-screen + first line: "everyone's panicking about this google update. don't." caption: "the latest google update, and why it's not the emergency your feed says it is. #marketing #seo #googleupdate #smallbusiness"
- **Don't:** react with no opinion, or chase a trend Jake has no real take on. the deadpan POV is the whole post. don't let it age into the evergreen rotation; this is a same-week unit.
- **Best for:** Commentary, Myth, FAQ. Subjects: marketing-news, SEO updates, AI-in-marketing.

## 2. Hook bank

The hook = on-screen text + spoken first line (clips) + caption first 150 chars, all carrying the keyword. Lowercase, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no em-dashes.

1. **The Diagnosis Two-Beat** — *Jake's signature; any teaching post.* Short claim, shorter twist.
   - "your product isn't the problem. your sequence is." · "slow sales aren't a traffic problem. they're a messaging problem." · "the logo was never the issue. the promise was."
2. **The Callout (symptom-first)** — *when you can name a wound the viewer recognizes.* Opens carousels and teardowns.
   - "the messaging mistake quietly costing you customers." · "the one line on your homepage losing the sale." · "this is why your best offer keeps getting ignored."
3. **The Contrarian Claim** — *when the content genuinely backs it; the brain won't leave the contradiction unresolved.*
   - "most bad marketing isn't bad. it's invisible." · "you don't need more traffic. you need a better first line." · "a rebrand will not fix your slow sales."
4. **The Number Cold-Open** — *when a real stat-bank figure carries the post.* Never first-person.
   - "a client went from 1.2% to 6.5% on one button change." · "3 crews to 9. same market, better sequence." · "17x roas came from one thing most teams skip."
5. **The List Tease** — *on count carousels; sets the swipe count and the curiosity gap.* Use specific/odd numbers.
   - "5 messaging mistakes quietly killing your conversion." · "3 things your value proposition has to do in one line." · "7 words that fix most weak headlines."
6. **The Mistake Warning** — *when the viewer fears they're guilty; pairs with pitfall content.*
   - "stop describing your service. you're losing the sale." · "if your headline names the service, you're doing it wrong." · "you're burying your best line in paragraph three."
7. **The Blunt Imperative** — *on tip clips and faceless posts; breaks the passive scroll.* Use for high-value tips.
   - "rewrite your headline as the wound, not the service." · "stop optimizing the logo. fix the promise." · "measure share of brand voice before you spend another dollar."
8. **The Question-as-Hook** — *on FAQ posts; doubles as a TikTok search query.*
   - "do you need more traffic, or better conversion." · "what's the last line of copy that actually stopped your scroll." · "is seo dead, or are you just doing it wrong."
9. **The Expert Explainer** — *on diagnosis clips; authority → curiosity → payoff in 3 seconds.* Credibility through scars, not first-person stats.
   - "twenty years in the trenches taught me this about slow sales." · "the hardest system to debug in marketing is people. here's why." · "i didn't learn this in a classroom. i learned it the hard way."
10. **The Aphorism** — *on quote cards and takeaways; high-save.* Use §7 lines verbatim.
    - "people browse intellectually and buy emotionally." · "content that doesn't convert is noise." · "anyone can get likes. revenue is the metric that matters."
11. **The Myth-Correction** — *on myth-bust carousels; state the belief, then strike it.*
    - "a new logo will fix slow sales. it won't." · "going viral builds a business. it doesn't, not by itself." · "more features means more sales. usually the opposite."
12. **The News React** — *on same-week commentary; rides trend demand.* C-tier, deadpan POV required.
    - "everyone's panicking about this google update. don't." · "tiktok is becoming the next google for discovery. here's the part people miss." · "another ai tool promising to replace marketers. it won't."

## 3. Tier index (for the dealer)

- **A (lead ~70% of draws):** One-Concept Carousel · Count Carousel · Teardown Carousel · Myth-Bust Carousel · Diagnosis Clip
- **B (solid, situational):** FAQ Answer Clip · Text-on-Screen Tip · Aphorism Card
- **C (experimental, ~30% quota):** News-React Clip

*Why:* the A cards sit on two stacked proofs — a documented TikTok mechanic (carousels = highest leverage; completion > views; contrarian/list/outcome hooks = top performers) AND a native Jake pattern (his back-catalog is teardowns, diagnosis-first captions, myth-busting, numbered how-tos). They carry the keyworded slide-1 + caption that wins TikTok SEO and map straight onto the messaging concepts. B cards are proven but narrower (the FAQ needs a common question; the tip teaches one small thing; the aphorism is a riff). The News-React belongs to the daily-reactive layer — keeping it C protects the feed from going all-reaction.

**Healthy weekly spread:** ~5 A draws (mostly carousels + at least one shot Diagnosis Clip), 2 B (one faceless, one FAQ/aphorism), 1 C react. Satisfies the format dial while staying carousel-first.
