how to frame your offer
8 ways to frame
the same offer.
most use one.
your product is fine. the way you frame it is doing all the losing. swipe.

1
new.
nobody has done it this way before. novelty buys you attention you have not earned yet.

2
only.
the one place they can get it. scarcity of source beats scarcity of time every day of the week.
@itsjakethewizard
3
big.
the outcome is larger than they expected. not more features. a bigger result.
@itsjakethewizard
4
fast.
they get it sooner than the alternative. speed is the easiest promise to prove and the hardest to argue with.
@itsjakethewizard
5
easy.
less work on their end. every step you remove is a reason to say yes you did not have to argue for.
@itsjakethewizard
6
safe.
the downside is covered. people do not buy the upside until they stop picturing the loss.
@itsjakethewizard
7
predictable.
it works the same way every time. boring is a feature when the last guy burned them.
@itsjakethewizard
8
simple.
they can explain it to someone else without you in the room. that is the one most people skip.
