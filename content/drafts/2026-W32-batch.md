### L1 · Mon 09:30 · `the-center-point` × Myth · Contrarian POV · A · hook: The Sacred Cow · + static card

AI is not making your marketing more creative. By default it does the opposite.

Everyone treats the tool as a creative partner. It is an aggregate. It takes your brief and returns the most probable answer from everything it has ever seen, and the most probable answer is the average one.

Feed the same model the same brief as your competitor and you get the same ad. Same middle. Different logo.

It can look like a leap. Take one unoriginal idea, wrap it in another, and the result reads as new. It is the same center in a costume.

A better prompt will not fix this. The sameness is what the machine is.

So bring the idea it could never have had. Put that at the center. Let the tool build the practical work underneath it.

Same tool, opposite result. The whole difference is who sits at the center.

What was the last idea in your marketing that came out of your head instead of a tool?

### L2 · Tue 10:00 · `the-keyword-pyramid` × Method · Process Carousel · A (9-slide doc) · hook: The Numbered Framework · RE-DEAL (missed twice)

You cannot rank the keyword you want first. That is not how the ladder works.

Everyone starts at the top. They pick the two-word term with the biggest volume, write one page for it, and wait. Nothing moves, so they write more pages for the same term.

More content aimed at a keyword you have no authority for is just a taller stack of pages nobody ranks.

The base comes first. Long-tail terms with a buyer's whole question in them. Low volume, low competition, and you can win them this quarter. Every one you win sends a signal about what you are actually about.

Then you point the base at the term you wanted, and the ladder holds weight.

Nine slides, in order. Do not skip ahead.

### L3 · Wed 10:00 · `pressure-is-not-a-plan` × Pitfall · Diagnosis-First Short · A · hook: The Callout

Most companies feel the pressure to adopt AI. Far fewer have written down who owns it.

Pressure is a mood. A plan is a decision.

Pasting into ChatGPT for your captions is a habit. It feels like adoption because something is happening every day. Nothing was decided.

A decision looks like this. One named owner. One list of what the tool is allowed to touch. One rule for what a human signs off before it ships.

### L5 · Fri 10:00 · `facebook-ads-arent-hard` × Tip · N-Things List · A · hook: The Numbered Framework · + static card · RE-DEAL (missed twice)

Four settings, not the ads. That is where beginners lose money on Facebook.

1. Underspending. You gave the algorithm forty dollars and three days and asked it to find your buyer. It never got out of the learning phase.

2. Targeting by income. You cannot buy intent with a demographic. Interests and behaviours beat a salary bracket every time.

3. The wrong objective. You optimised for traffic, so the algorithm found you people who click and leave. It did exactly what you asked.

4. An ad that does not match the page it lands on. The promise changes between the click and the screen, and the visitor decides they were tricked.

None of those four are the creative. Fix them, then let the creative do the work it was always going to do.

Which of the four is running on your account right now?
