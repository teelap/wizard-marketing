### Classic marketing canon (Jake's evergreen IP — backfilled 2026-06-15)

*The four concepts above are all AI-reactive. **The Eight Dominoes (the book's spine; Domino 02 Attention Catalyst already lives as a draft):**
- **Sequence beats components** — a pen topples a skyscraper in 29 steps; the gap is order, not volume; same crew, same stone, different chain (the-domino-effect)
- **Core Attraction (01)** — people buy emotionally; you can't out-optimize the wrong emotion; diagnose the appeal, don't choose it (core-attraction)
- **Value Proposition (03)** — two promises (why buy / why keep reading); specificity is risk and risk signals confidence; the swap test (the-sixty-second-promise)
- **Impact Chain (04)** — feature → benefit → benefit of the benefit → cost → cost of the cost; the cost of inaction is where urgency lives (the-impact-chain)
- **Mechanism (05)** — name your process and become unreplicable; the barbecue test; a mediocre name beats no name (name-your-mechanism)
- **Barrier Breakdown (06)** — the objection is never the objection; break the belief underneath, before it's spoken (the-objection-is-never-the-objection)
- **Embracing Limitations (07)** — the flaw you hide is the differentiator you're missing; celebrate-or-fix (embrace-the-limitation)
- **New Opportunity (08)** — stop improving the old way, replace it; depositioning; improvement compares, a new opportunity converts (replace-the-category)

**Signature frameworks (very ownable, very Jake):**
- **Offer Amplifiers** — eight frames for any offer (New/Only/Big/Fast/Easy/Safe/Predictable/Simple); pick one or two true ones (the-eight-offer-amplifiers)
- **Selling the Sword** — the customer is the hero, you're the sword; hero/dragon/reward/sword (selling-the-sword)
- **Why this, then why you** — every sale answers two questions in order; most skip the demand question (why-this-then-why-you)
- **Brand before logo** — doctrine, messaging, positioning come before the logo; the logo is the last decision (brand-before-logo)
- **T-shaped marketer** — broad knowledge, one deep skill; flat lines get automated/cut first (the-t-shaped-marketer)

**Tactical bread-and-butter (with current 2026 stats):**
- **CRO / surgical tweaks** — convert the traffic you have; the 1.2%→6.5% button; F-shape scan; click budget (the-surgical-tweak)
- **DSP / omnichannel** — single-channel is dead; show up everywhere; ~17x ROAS; programmatic ≈ 90% of digital display (show-up-everywhere)
- **Focus over spread** — one good channel beats five half-assed; the road to omnichannel, not its opposite (one-good-channel)
- **Vanity metrics vs revenue** — anyone can get likes; content that doesn't convert is noise; ROEMI (likes-are-not-revenue)
- **Personal brand as exit-proof asset** — they can't take your audience away; people do business with people (the-exit-proof-asset)

### Traditional / channel tactics (the operational layer — backfilled 2026-06-15)

*The how-to bread-and-butter Jake asked for: SEO, websites, analytics, paid, local, email, content ops. Mined verbatim from the video knowledge base (steps, client examples, catchphrases). Skips ground already pillared on the Grimoire (NAP, GBP-as-its-own-post, reviews, SEO+PPC, TikTok SEO) — those are cross-referenced instead.*

- **SEO is a marathon** — not a sprint; the site is a living digital salesperson you maintain like a car; stop and rankings decay; difficulty/authority/relevancy set the timeline; "stay in your lane" (seo-is-a-marathon)
- **Search intent** — the 4 keyword intents (navigational/informational/commercial/transactional); match the page format to the intent or rank for nothing (search-intent)
- **Keyword pyramid** — build the long-tail base first, the head term falls; win the 11-30 rankings already in Search Console; "anything off page one might as well not exist" (the-keyword-pyramid)
- **Speak Google's language** — one H1, never skip a header level, schema (local + FAQ), entity maps + provable facts; same structure gets you cited by AI (speak-googles-language)
- **Backlinks** — other sites vouching for you; the 4 ways (guest posts, listicles, HARO, press releases) + the skyscraper move; you ask, you don't wait (build-backlinks)
- **Page intent** — every page has one of 4 jobs; transactional pages are hypercritical; answer "what is this / why am I here / what next"; service pages not blogs (page-intent)
- **Website fundamentals** — load fast (or you don't have a website), mobile-first (Google indexes mobile), the three-question clarity test, the 5 WordPress setup mistakes (your-website-has-three-jobs)
- **GA4 + conversion tracking** — marketing returns results AND data; the four report areas; set a conversion event on the thank-you page; schedule the report; heat maps for the "why" (ga4-and-conversion-tracking)
- **Facebook ads** — the 4 beginner mistakes (underspend, income-targeting, wrong objective, ad-page mismatch); creative beats targeting; fail fast; lookalikes 1/1-3/3-5; install the pixel now (facebook-ads-arent-hard)
- **Google Ads swipe file** — go to Google, pretend you're a customer, copy the ads still running (they're proven winners); don't reinvent, collect (the-swipe-file)
- **Don't waste your ad budget** — the platform wants to spend it all; turn off display/partner networks, phrase not broad match, ad schedule, negative keywords; "your ads serve you, not Google" (dont-waste-your-ad-budget)
- **Four-prong local play** — GBP + a service page per service + reviews + a ~$15/day ad drip; geo-keywords + city-in-testimonial; "they can't buy you if they don't know you exist" (the-four-prong-local-play)
- **The list you own** — email is the one audience the algorithm can't throttle; collect on purpose, trade something free, feed it from repurposed content (the-list-you-own)
- **1-4-10 content system** — one long-form → a few medium → 7-9 short each; one big piece beats a hundred little ones; repurpose, don't reinvent (the-1-4-10-content-system)
- **Social search** — buyers search TikTok/YouTube/Instagram for products, places, how-tos (49% have used TikTok as search, ~65% of Gen Z); optimize to be FOUND not just watched (keyword spoken + caption + on-screen text), topical authority, engagement; honest framing = a surging UNDER-LEVERAGED surface (~8% of journeys start on social), NOT "social beat Google" (Google still most-trusted; AI is the bigger disruptor) (social-search)
