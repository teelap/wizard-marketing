# Instagram — Rulebook (2026)

**Priority:** ★★ Real effort (best owned-reach surface of the Meta apps; carousel-first matches our strength)
**One-line strategy:** Eight Dominoes diagnosis carousels engineered for shares (DM sends drive reach).

## Cadence
- **4–5 feed posts/wk** (consistency ≈ 5x the engagement of sporadic). Spread across days — bundling same-day suppresses reach.
- Best windows (US): **Wed 12pm, Wed 6pm, Thu 9am.**

## Formats (ranked)
1. **Carousels** — highest engagement (~10% vs ~6–7% for stills/Reels); strongest for saves/sends.
2. **Reels** — the discovery engine (most non-follower views).
3. **Static** — lowest.
- **Emerging:** hybrid carousels (mix stills with 3–5s video clips per slide → more dwell); add audio to a carousel to make it Reels-feed eligible.

## Algorithm / discovery
- Top signals: **sends-per-reach** (DM shares — heaviest weight), watch time, **saves**, likes-per-reach.
- **SEO > hashtags:** front-load the primary keyword in the first 1–2 caption lines + bio (public posts now index in Google).
- Carousel mechanic: **unswiped slides get resurfaced later as "new"** → multiple distribution passes. Newer (Feb 2026): carousels with high swipe-through get a **second push to non-followers ~48–72h after posting**.
- **Originality / provenance weighting (2026):** Instagram is weighting "raw, real, human" content over AI-generated material; **original content reportedly gets 40–60% more distribution than reposts**, and accounts publishing **10+ reposts in 30 days can be excluded from recommendations entirely**. *Checked against our spine:* "repost" here means re-sharing **other people's** content via the Repost feature, not re-uploading your own archive footage — and our own data agrees, since back-catalog spine reels took 2.11x and 1.21x the IG median in W32. **No change to the spine. Re-check if IG reach drops without another cause.**

## Specs
- Reels/vertical 1080×1920 (9:16); **carousel 1080×1350 (4:5)**; Reels 30–90s (≤3min), hook in 3s, text overlays (~50% watch muted), **no third-party watermarks** (suppressed).
- Carousels up to 20 slides; **6–10 optimal.** **3–5 hashtags** max (search-assist, not reach). One link in bio → use a link-hub.

## Highest-leverage move
Every Eight Dominoes idea as an **8–10 slide diagnosis-first carousel** (slide 1 = the symptom/failure, last = the fix + book pre-order). Engineer the final slide as a **"send this to your CMO"** share prompt — sends drive non-follower reach.

## Repurpose from
TikTok scripts → carousel slide copy · TikTok videos → Reels · book chapters → framework carousels.
