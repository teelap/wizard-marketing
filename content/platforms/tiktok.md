# TikTok — Rulebook (2026)

**Handle:** @itsjakethewizard · **Priority:** ★★★ Primary (110K followers — his real audience)
**One-line strategy:** carousel-first for the book launch; keep posting velocity high; win TikTok SEO.

## Cadence
- **4–7/wk (~1/day)** is the growth sweet spot; Jake's proven ~2x/day still works. **Cut weak posts, not cadence.**
- Engagement velocity in the **first 1–24h** is a top signal — post when he can reply early.
- Timing (US B2B): **Tue–Thu**, 12–1pm and 4–5pm ET.

## Formats (ranked)
1. **Photo carousels / Photo Mode** — highest leverage. ~81% more engagement, ~82% more likes than video; each swipe is its own dwell signal. *Maps perfectly to our AI pipeline.*
2. **Talking-head diagnosis clips, 21–34s** — Jake's deadpan voice travels.
3. **Text-on-screen / screen-recording tips** — faceless works.
4. **Longer thought-leadership, 1–3 min** — now beats 15s clips on watch time.
- **Emerging:** faceless framework content; AI carousels + text/image-to-video (TikTok Symphony, Seedance); anti-polish "reali-TEA" authenticity (on-brand for "anti-magic").

## Algorithm / discovery
- **Watch time / completion > views.** Hook in the **first 3 seconds**.
- **TikTok SEO is a direct ranking metric** — it transcribes audio and reads on-screen text. 43% of top search results come from sub-10K accounts, so it's winnable.
- Put **2–3 target keywords** spoken aloud + in on-screen text + the first 150 caption chars. **3–5 hashtags** (evergreen + trending). **Saves and shares** weigh heavily.
- **Follower-first distribution (2026):** a new video is test-shown to a slice of your **followers** before strangers; their completion/shares/saves decide whether it goes wider. The reach ladder starts at the warm base.
- **Topic consistency is now a reach signal** *(refresh 2026-08, secondary sources — treat as a hypothesis, not a rule)*: accounts spreading across **3+ unrelated topics reportedly see ~45% lower reach** than topic-consistent ones. **Live tension with our design:** the video spine deliberately deals a different topic every day off a 1,025-clip catalog (web-design → seo → cro → paid-ads → analytics → sales → local). Jake's TikTok numbers show no penalty so far (spine reels at 1.00x and 1.26x median), so **do not narrow the deck on this** — but if TikTok reach softens without another explanation, test clustering a week's reels into one topic family before blaming the content.

## Specs
- 1080×1920, 9:16, MP4/MOV; carousels up to 35 images. Keep content out of the **bottom 15%** (UI bar).
- Lengths: 11–18s (punchy), 21–34s (story/demo), 30–60s+ (educational). Caption first 150 chars = the hook; lowercase is fine. **Link in bio only.**
