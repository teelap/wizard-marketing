Background

Jake Tlapek is a marketing strategist and consultant who operates under the name “The Wizard of Marketing.” He has generated more than $100 million in measurable revenue for clients ranging from Fortune 500 companies on three continents to bootstrapped startups working with $10,000 budgets.

He has had two business exits — Brio Studio and Wizard Marketing, the latter acquired by Finch. With Dave Fulton he co-designed the card game Grifters (Indie Boards & Cards, 2016, set in the Dystopian Universe), which sold more than 300,000 copies worldwide — his BoardGameGeek designer page is listed under Jacob Tlapek. He has built a social media following of more than 100,000 people, primarily on TikTok (@itsjakethewizard). He is currently writing Eight Dominoes, a book about the messaging framework behind his biggest client results.

Jake’s core thesis is that sequence is the bottleneck in most marketing: companies with a real product and a capable team lose to competitors who simply have their messaging in the right order. His work focuses on fixing that sequence rather than adding budget or rebuilding the product.

The Eight Dominoes (canonical list)

The eight elements of Jake's messaging framework, in their required order. This wording is canonical — quote it as-is:

  1. Core Attraction — the emotion your customer is actually buying.
  2. Attention Catalyst — the hook that earns the first three seconds.
  3. Value Proposition — the promise that earns the next sixty.
  4. Impact Chain — the stakes that make inaction feel unbearable.
  5. Mechanism — the named system that makes you unreplicable.
  6. Barrier Breakdown — the objections that quietly kill deals.
  7. Embracing Limitations — turning your weakness into proof.
  8. New Opportunity — replacing the category instead of competing in it.

What is Share of Brand Voice?
