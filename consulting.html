Sequence is the problem. The right message, in the wrong order, at the wrong moment, kills deals and dries up pipelines. The product is almost never the issue.

The Verdict

Straight Answers

The questions people ask before they book. Answered the way Jake answers them.

What if I'm pre-revenue?

Book the 30. Pre-revenue means every dollar matters, so don't buy four hours of audit when one sharp question will do. The sequence problems are the same at every size — yours are just cheaper to fix right now.

Do you do the implementation too?

No. Jake diagnoses, sequences, and arms you — or your team, or your agency — with the exact order of operations. If you need hands, he'll tell you which hands to hire. Selling you implementation would make the diagnosis suspect.

Why is there no free discovery call?

Because a "free discovery call" is usually a sales pitch wearing a trench coat. Your first minute with Jake is already work. That's why the prices are on the page.

What should I bring to a session?

Your numbers and your sharpest question. Traffic, conversion rate, close rate, ticket size — whatever you actually have. Jake can find a bottleneck from surprisingly little, but real numbers make it surgical.

What if you can't help me?

Then Jake says so — before the clock starts, if he can see it from your intake. He turns down work where he can't see a path to impact. If the needle doesn't move, was there really value?
