Alice Video Assistant

A private assistant operated by Jake Tlapek, Jake the Wizard, to help him find and reuse existing assets in his configured Google Drive library and inspect his YouTube channel through his private Alice conversation.

Find and save selected files

Alice can browse file details in the configured Drive folder and its verified subfolders. When requested, she can save a selected original file or export a supported Google document, spreadsheet, presentation or drawing for a later task. Saved copies retain their source details. The connection does not change the originals or their sharing permissions.

Check the channel and its videos

A separate authorization lets Alice inspect the configured YouTube channel and its uploaded-video inventory, including titles, descriptions, dates, status and available statistics. This uses YouTube API Services. It does not download YouTube media, retrieve transcripts, upload, edit or publish videos.

The app is for Jake's private use and has no public registration. Google sign-in and consent control access to the selected accounts. Relevant results and saved content may be processed by the assistant's configured service providers, as described in the privacy policy.
