THE
BRUTAL
TRUTH.

I didn't learn marketing in a classroom. I learned it in the trenches, making mistakes that cost real money and figuring out what drives revenue. I started in the Air Force as an IT Tech, learning how complex systems work. Twenty years of marketing taught me the hardest system to debug is people.

I've made two exits: Brio Studio and Wizard Marketing, which was acquired by Finch where I served as Chief Evangelist. Now I'm back out on my own, consulting directly with founders and teams who are done with theory. Along the way I watched the same pattern play out across every industry I touched: good businesses losing to worse ones because the sequence of their message was wrong. That pattern became Eight Dominoes, my upcoming book.

Somewhere in there I also co-designed a card game, Grifters, that sold 300,000 copies. Different shelf, same lesson: the product is never the problem. The sequence you present it in is.

I've generated over $100 million in measurable revenue for clients spanning Fortune 500 companies on three continents. If you want honest strategy from someone who's earned his insights through bruises, not blog posts, you're in the right room.
