THE
BRUTAL
TRUTH.

I didn't learn marketing in a classroom. I learned it in the trenches — making mistakes that cost real money and figuring out what actually drives revenue. I started in the Air Force as an IT Tech, learning the raw architecture of systems. But the real game is human psychology.

I've made two exits — Brio Studio and Wizard Marketing, which was acquired by Finch where I served as Chief Evangelist. Now I'm back out on my own, consulting directly with founders and teams who are done with theory. Along the way I watched the same pattern play out across every industry I touched: good businesses losing to worse ones because the sequence of their message was wrong. That pattern became Eight Dominoes, my upcoming book.

I've generated over $100 million in measurable revenue for clients spanning Fortune 500 companies on three continents. If you want honest strategy from someone who's earned his insights through bruises — not blog posts — you've found the right place.
