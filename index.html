About Jake

The brutal truth.

I didn’t learn marketing in a classroom. I learned it in the trenches, making mistakes that cost real money and figuring out what drives revenue. I started in the Air Force as an IT Tech, learning how complex systems work. Thirteen years of marketing taught me the hardest system to debug is people.

I’ve made two exits: Brio Studio and Wizard Marketing, an SEO and web shop I built over seven years and then sold. Now I’m back out on my own, consulting directly with founders and teams who are done with theory. Along the way I watched the same pattern play out across every industry I touched: good businesses losing to worse ones because they sold the product while the competitor sold the dream. That pattern became Eight Dominoes, my book — out now.

Somewhere in there I also co-designed a published card game, Grifters (Indie Boards & Cards). Different shelf, same lesson: people don’t buy the box. They buy the night they’ll have around the table.

I’ve generated over $100 million in measurable revenue for clients. If you want honest strategy from someone who’s earned his insights through bruises, not blog posts, you’re in the right room.
