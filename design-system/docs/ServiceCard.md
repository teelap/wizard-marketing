Offer card: coloured top band, illustrated icon on its edge, price pill, title, two lines of copy and an all-caps call to action.

- One card per offer, three across. Each offer keeps its colour and icon: consulting red + `consulting`, Mastermind teal + `mastermind`, the book yellow + `dominoes`, podcast pink + `podcast`, tools mint + `tools`, contact sky + `contact`.

```jsx
<ServiceCard title="Private consulting" color="red" icon="consulting" tag="From $400" cta="Book a session" href="/consulting">
  One-on-one strategy. Find the bottleneck, fix the message, compound the results.
</ServiceCard>
```
