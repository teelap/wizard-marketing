# Social posts and other formats

All Jake the Wizard social posts use this brand. Every format is built from the same parts: cream ground, a rainbow bend or arch framing the edge, one big Fraunces headline with one emphasised word, ink-outlined cards, and Jake in a TV frame when a photo is used.

## Sizes

| Format | Size (px) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel slide (Instagram, LinkedIn document) | 1080 × 1350 | Rainbow bend down the left and along the bottom; slide number in a coin top-right |
| Square card | 1080 × 1080 | One idea, one headline, one detail |
| Story, Reel or TikTok cover | 1080 × 1920 | Keep text out of the top 220px and bottom 380px (app UI) |
| Share image (Open Graph) | 1200 × 630 | Kicker pill, headline, hat mark, jakethewizard.com pill |
| LinkedIn banner | 1584 × 396 | Keep the left 400px clear of the profile photo |
| YouTube banner | 2560 × 1440 | Safe area 1546 × 423, centred |
| X banner | 1500 × 500 | |
| Facebook cover | 851 × 315 | |
| Profile photo | 1080 × 1080 | Jake's photo inside the badge's starburst ring |

## Rules for every post

- One idea per post. The first slide or frame states it plainly; a stranger should get it without the caption.
- Headline in Fraunces Black, 6 to 10 words, ending with a full stop; one word gets a yellow `Highlight` or a `RainbowWord`.
- Body text at least 36px on a 1080-wide canvas. Never more than about 30 words on one slide.
- The last slide carries one order-style call to action and the handle @itsjakethewizard or jakethewizard.com.
- Use only approved numbers. No emoji, no exclamation marks.
- Never make a cartoon or AI likeness of Jake; use the rainbow-wall photos.
