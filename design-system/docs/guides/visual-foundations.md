# Visual foundations

The look is 70s Schoolhouse Rock: cream paper, thick ink outlines with hard offset shadows, rainbow bends and arches, starbursts, TV-framed photos and rainbow letters. It comes from Jake's "Hold the Vision" summit deck and is the one Jake the Wizard brand for everything: the website, slides, social posts, banners, emails.

Eight Dominoes, the book, has its own separate brand (yellow and ink, the real cover dominoes) on its own site. Don't mix it into Jake the Wizard designs. The book can appear here as a product, set in this brand.

## Colour

- Ground: `--cream` (#FFF1D6) for pages; `--paper` (#FFFAF0) inside cards, fields and pills.
- Ink: `--ink` (#1B1A17) for all text, outlines and shadows. Small red text uses `--red-ink` (#B3241F); `--red` is for fills and marks.
- Brand colours for fills: `--yellow` #FFC928 (the main action and highlights), `--teal` #12A4A4, `--pink` #F6A8CB, `--red` #E5322B, `--salmon` #FF9473, `--mint` #91DE87, `--sky` #5DB6EC, `--orange` #FF7A33, `--maroon` #8B1D3D, `--forest` #17271F, `--navy` #3C3F9F.
- The rainbow is always nine stripes in this order, in an ink casing: teal, pink, red, salmon, yellow, forest, mint, maroon, sky.
- One coloured band per screen (a yellow, teal, sky or pink section); everything else sits on cream.
- On red, maroon, forest and navy fills, text is cream.

## Type

- Display: Fraunces Black, the soft and wonky cut (SOFT 100, WONK 1, opsz 144). Headlines, card titles, buttons, numbers. Tight leading (.98), slight negative tracking.
- Body: Nunito 700 at 18px, line height 1.55. Bold is 900.
- Labels: Nunito 900, uppercase, 12 to 13px, tracking .12 to .14em (kickers, pills, field labels).
- Typewriter: Courier Prime 700 for form inputs, excerpts and dates.
- Marker: Permanent Marker for hand-written bits only (check marks, grades, cassette labels).
- Headlines end with a full stop. One emphasised word per headline: a yellow `Highlight`, a `RainbowWord` or a `Pop` outline.

## Shape and depth

- Outlines: 3px on pills and small controls, 4px on cards and buttons, 5 to 6px on photos and big art.
- Shadows are hard and offset down-right in ink, never blurred: 4 to 6px on buttons and coins, 8 to 10px on cards, 14px on photo frames.
- Corners: pills fully round; cards 18 to 20px; photo TVs 36px.
- A slight tilt (1 to 2 degrees) on photos, tiles and report cards makes things feel hand-placed. Alternate the direction.
- A film-grain texture sits over the whole page.

## Motion

70s cartoon timing: rainbows paint on, TVs switch on with a flicker, rainbow letters hop, starbursts and records spin slowly, buttons squash and stretch on hover. Everything stops for reduced-motion users, and nothing is ever hidden while it waits to animate.

## Icons

- The six illustrated icons (consulting, mastermind, dominoes, podcast, tools, contact) mark offers and destinations.
- Font Awesome 6 for small UI glyphs (arrows, menu, social logos).
- No emoji.
