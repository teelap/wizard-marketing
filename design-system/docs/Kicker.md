Small all-caps label pill that sits above a heading and says what the section is ("Consulting · Mastermind · Book").

- Two to five plain words. Separate items with a middle dot.
- Solid ink on cream or paper; `light` on yellow, teal, sky or pink grounds.

```jsx
<Kicker>Jake Tlapek · The Wizard of Marketing</Kicker>
<Kicker light>Free to join · Membership $500/mo</Kicker>
```
