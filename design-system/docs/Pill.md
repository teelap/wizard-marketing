Small all-caps tag with an ink outline and a brand-colour fill.

- For formats, statuses and short facts: "Paperback", "Kindle", "Free on Kindle Unlimited". Use `dashed` for things that aren't out yet ("Hardcover & audiobook on the way").
- Vary colours across a row (yellow, sky, pink, mint).

```jsx
<Pill color="yellow">Paperback</Pill>
<Pill color="sky">Kindle</Pill>
<Pill dashed>Hardcover &amp; audiobook on the way</Pill>
```
