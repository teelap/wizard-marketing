Display word with a thick ink outline and a hard drop shadow, like cartoon lettering. Use for big numbers and single words.

- Best on a coloured ground with `color="paper"` or `color="cream"`, or for numbers like "01".
- Doesn't wrap, so keep it to one word or number.

```jsx
<Pop color="paper">01</Pop>
<Display size="xl"><Pop color="yellow">110K+</Pop></Display>
```
