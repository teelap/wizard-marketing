Alice Video Assistant

Privacy policy

Alice Video Assistant is operated by Jake Tlapek, Jake the Wizard, for his own configured Google Drive library and YouTube channel. It has no public registration. This notice covers the video connection, separately from Alice's email, calendar and other tools and the website's business services.

Google information accessed

Google Drive and YouTube are authorized separately through Google's sign-in and consent screens. The app does not receive Jake's Google password. It verifies the connected account, configured Drive folder and selected YouTube channel. Its read permissions cover more of the authorized accounts than the app's folder and channel checks.

Drive results include file and folder names, IDs, types, dates, sizes, links and folder relationships. When Jake requests a saved file, the app can download the selected original or export a supported Google document, spreadsheet, presentation or drawing. It can retain that content and its source details for later tasks. Downloads and exports are subject to the app's size and format limits. These reads do not change Google's originals or sharing permissions.

YouTube results include channel details and uploaded-video metadata such as titles, descriptions, tags, dates, duration, availability and publishing status, and available statistics. The app does not download YouTube media, retrieve transcripts, upload, edit or publish videos. It uses YouTube API Services. Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Purpose and service providers

Information is used to answer Jake's questions, locate assets, inspect channel activity and carry out his requested tasks. Relevant results, selected file content and conversation context can be supplied to the configured ChatGPT/OpenAI assistant. Context supplied to a separately assigned Claude task is processed by Anthropic. Private Telegram conversations are also handled by Telegram. These providers may retain the information they receive under their applicable account settings and privacy practices; processing is not wholly local.

Google data is not sold or used for advertising or credit decisions. The app does not use it to train a general-purpose AI model. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs are subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

Private operational files hold OAuth credentials and connection records. Results can remain in conversation and task history. Saved originals and exports can remain in Alice's reusable-file storage and linked notes, together with source details. Separate assistant tools may reuse a saved file for a task Jake authorizes.

Google API requests use HTTPS, and local service access controls protect private state. Daily ledger backups are encrypted and exclude credentials; they can include saved file content. The configured rotation keeps the latest 30 recognized daily copies locally and in Jake's Google Drive backup folder. This is a copy-count limit, not a promise that every copy disappears after 30 calendar days or that every live file and provider history is encrypted at rest.

Retention and removal

Credentials support the connection until replaced, revoked or removed. Saved files and operational history support continuity until removed. The app currently has no automatic age-based purge covering all saved files, conversations, linked notes, provider histories and backups.

The video disconnect control stops future video reads and removes the local video credentials and connection records it manages, while attempting Google revocation. It covers the paired Drive and YouTube video connections. It does not erase previously imported reusable files and linked notes, assistant histories, mixed reports, backups, older Telegram messages or copies held by other providers. Removing those copies requires separate cleanup and the relevant services' controls.

Jake can also withdraw access through Google's account permissions settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase information already received. Google revocation can affect other grants in the same app project. Local removal does not delete the original Drive files or YouTube channel content.

For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact jake@jakethewizard.com. This notice makes no fixed deadline or guarantee of automatic deletion across every retained copy.
